# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Genshi exists to explore, define, and evolve design system infrastructure.

We welcome participation from designers, engineers, researchers, architects, product leaders, and anyone interested in the discipline of design systems.

We are committed to creating a respectful, constructive, and collaborative environment.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with professionalism and respect.
- Critique ideas rather than individuals.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to challenge.
- Support evidence-based discussion.
- Share knowledge openly and constructively.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for contributors of varying backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Trolling, inflammatory behavior, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated dismissal of contributors without engaging with the substance of their contributions.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact maintainers through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (subject line: "Code of Conduct") or GitHub private reporting channels.

Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.

## Enforcement Principles

Maintainers are responsible for protecting the health of the community.

Responses may include:

- Clarification of expectations
- Requests to modify behavior
- Temporary restrictions
- Removal of participation privileges

Enforcement decisions will prioritize community safety, fairness, and the long-term health of the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Repository discussions
- Issues
- Pull requests
- Project communication channels
- Community events associated with Genshi

## A Shared Responsibility

A healthy framework community depends on thoughtful participation.

Every contributor helps shape the culture of the project.
