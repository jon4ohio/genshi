# Principle

## Definition

A normative belief of the framework or design system, expressed as a falsifiable statement with testable implications.

## Why It Matters

Principles set non-negotiable interpretation boundaries. Without them, every debate reopens foundational questions.

## Relationships

- **framework** — principles are part of the framework's intellectual base
- **decision** — decisions derive from and must not contradict accepted principles
- **governance** — principles inform what governance must protect
- **design-system** — adopters may endorse Genshi principles or declare explicit divergence via decision

## Common Misinterpretations

- Treating principles as slogans without implications or tests
- Confusing principles with decisions (beliefs vs binding choices)
- Treating principles as immutable when implications require revision—principles change through governed process, not silent edits

## Genshi Interpretation

Genshi maintains six stable principles in `foundation/principles/`. Each cites manifesto sections. Principles are entities in the metamodel with lifecycle states.
