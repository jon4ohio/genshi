# Capability

## Definition

An organizational ability that a design system enables—such as consistent accessibility, faster compliant delivery, or reduced experience risk—distinct from the artifacts that evidence it.

## Why It Matters

Executives fund capabilities and risk reduction, not Storybook story counts. Naming capability separately prevents confusing outputs with outcomes.

## Relationships

- **design-system** — produces and sustains capabilities for the organization
- **artifact** — evidences capability; does not substitute for it
- **governance** — protects capabilities from erosion through exceptions and drift
- **framework** — helps adopters map design system investment to capability models

## Common Misinterpretations

- Using "capability" to mean a software feature or npm package
- Claiming capability exists because artifacts were published (publication ≠ adoption)
- Treating capability as purely design-team scope, ignoring engineering and product outcomes

## Genshi Interpretation

Capability is organizational, not technical. Conformance levels (Aligned, Governed, Operational) describe increasing organizational capability, not repository completeness.
