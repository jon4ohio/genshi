# Practice

## Definition

A repeatable operational method—such as a workshop, audit, or review cadence—that applies governance and lifecycle to entities without replacing principles or decisions.

## Why It Matters

Organizations need how-we-run discipline distinct from what-we-commit-to. Practices operationalize governance without becoming undocumented canon.

## Relationships

- **governance** — practices support governance capability
- **assessment** — practices may produce assessment evidence
- **artifact** — practices apply to artifact and capability entities
- **principle** — practices must not substitute for principles

## Common Misinterpretations

- Elevating team rituals to binding canon without decision backing
- Confusing practice (method) with Practice as mandatory framework law for all adopters
- Treating practices as replacements for ADRs

## Genshi Interpretation

Practices may be documented in `operations/` or `foundation/governance/`. They are metamodel entities distinct from Principles and Decisions. Framework practices are advisory unless adopted via decision.
