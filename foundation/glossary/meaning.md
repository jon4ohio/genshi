# Meaning

## Definition

The shared semantic intent that design system artifacts encode and that the organization commits to preserve across tools, teams, and scale.

## Why It Matters

Scale without preserved meaning produces duplicate artifacts, ambiguous tokens, and contradictory guidance. Meaning is the property the system exists to protect.

## Relationships

- **token** — token semantics document meaning for specific design dimensions
- **decision** — decisions anchor meaning to recorded rationale
- **knowledge-asset** — carriers of committed meaning
- **interpretive-debt** — measures divergence from intended meaning
- **principle** — Principle 06 (meaning survives scale) states the normative requirement

## Common Misinterpretations

- Reducing meaning to naming conventions or token labels alone
- Assuming meaning is preserved because files were copied across tools
- Confusing meaning (organizational intent) with aesthetic preference

## Genshi Interpretation

Meaning is a **primary objective**, not a metamodel entity. Semantic discipline means one concept maps to one canonical expression with explicit aliases and recorded exceptions. "Semantic health" in assessments refers to meaning preservation, not inventory size.
