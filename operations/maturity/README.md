## Levels (summary)

Maturity levels are isomorphic to adopter conformance levels:

| Level | Trust type | Primary certification |
|-------|------------|----------------------|
| **Aligned** | Decision trust | Stable token semantics decision-backed |
| **Governed** | Structural trust | Lifecycle, ownership, invalid-state discipline |
| **Operational** | Meaning and capability trust | Meaning preserved at scale; capability evidenced |

Programs below Aligned minimum evidence make **no maturity level claim**. See [framework.md § Below Aligned](framework.md#below-aligned).
